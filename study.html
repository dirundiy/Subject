The method

Completing the square

A perfect square trinomial always looks like this:

x² + bx + (b/2)² = (x + b/2)²

x² + bx is the square plus two strips. The dashed corner, (b/2)², is the piece you add to “complete” the square.

Steps for x² + bx + c

  1. Take half of b.
  2. Square it. That's the constant.
  3. Add and subtract it right after the bx term.
  4. Factor the first three terms as (x + b/2)² and combine the leftover numbers.

Even b

x² + 10x + 7 half of 10 = 5, 5² = 25 x² + 10x + 25 − 25 + 7 = (x + 5)² − 18

Odd b

x² + 7x + 2 half of 7 = 72, squared = 494 x² + 7x + 494 − 494 + 84 = (x + 72)² − 414

Watch out

  • The sign inside matches b: x² − 6x → (x − 3)².
  • The constant (b/2)² is always positive.
  • Odd b: keep fractions, skip decimals. Write c over 4 to combine (2 = 8/4).
  • You add and subtract, so the value doesn't change.

Not on the test: passé récent (venir de + infinitive) and double object pronouns. Skip those parts of the review sheets.
